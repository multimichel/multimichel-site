The scenic route

I’m Michel Ferreira. Brazilian, Sydney-based, Designer Advocate at Figma. I’ve been building things on the web since 1998, which is long enough to have been wrong about most of it at least once.

I got here the long way around: an Apple II at eight years old, design agencies in Brazil and California, then Booking.com in Amsterdam, Shopify in Canada, Atlassian and Xero in Sydney. Five countries, one job title that keeps changing shape.

Besides the work

Husband, father of three, full-time goofball, and — on the record — still available for the NBA draft.

I host Latinxs Who Design, a podcast about Latinx designers thriving in this industry. I teach a portfolio and career course, and I’ve mentored more than a hundred designers across ten-odd countries — through MentorCruise, ADPList, and as a Design Leadership ambassador with Echos.

I write when something has been bothering me long enough to become an argument. Most of it ends up at A List Apart or on Medium.
