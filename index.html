Olá & Welcome

I’m Michel. I’ve been designing digital products for nearly 30 years, through agencies, Booking.com, Shopify, Atlassian and Xero, and today as a Designer Advocate at Figma.

I wear a lot of hats — and I don’t mean my extensive baseball cap collection. The one thing that’s stayed the same is that I care about the whole product.

I care about whether we’re asking the right question and solving something that matters to people. I care about how products connect, how teams make decisions, and whether the systems underneath them help or get in the way. And I care about what finally shows up on the screen — the interaction, the component, the type, the spacing, the pixels.

Because the person using the product might never see most of those decisions. They feel all of them.
